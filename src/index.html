Transcription CPU
Photos and PDFs are read by a Python optical music recognition service running on the CPU — no graphics card needed. It is a best-effort transcription: check the result against your scan before trusting the audio.
About the sound

Every instrument is physically modelled synthesis built in Web Audio — inharmonic piano strings, hammer transients, bowed vibrato, wind breath. Nothing is streamed or downloaded, which is why the whole app fits in one file and works offline.
