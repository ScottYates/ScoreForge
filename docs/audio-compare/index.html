If A and B sound the same, the recording survives the conversion and anything you hear missing from the instrument pack is the pack, not the codec.

What is deliberately not happening

  • No oscillator, no additive or FM synthesis, no generated waveform. The sound is decoded PCM from a file that was recorded.
  • No attack/sustain/release envelope. Nothing is connected between the source node and the speakers but the source itself.
  • No looping, loop = false, and no loop points exist in either file — there is nothing to invent them from.
  • No stop(). The sample is started at its first sample and left to finish on its own, at whatever length the recording is.
  • No gain change. The level you hear is the level in the recording; the WAV peaks at full scale as it stands.
  • playbackRate = 1 and detune = 0: the file plays at the pitch it was recorded at.
  • Every click makes a new AudioBufferSourceNode and nothing is ever stopped, so repeated clicks overlap instead of cutting each other off.
